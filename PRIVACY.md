# Privacy Policy for TrueMath Innovator

**Last updated:** September 30, 2026

This policy explains what data TrueMath Innovator ("the app") handles when you use it.

## 1. Who we are

TrueMath Innovator is developed by Sajid Abro.
Contact: dijasniassuh8@gmail.com

## 2. Data we do not collect

The developer does not collect, store or sell your personal information. The app does not require an account or login, and we do not run our own servers that store your data.

## 3. Data that is sent to third-party services

Some features need the internet. When you use them, the text you enter is sent directly from your device to the following services:

| Feature | Service | What is sent |
|---|---|---|
| AI answers | Groq (groq.com) or OpenRouter (openrouter.ai) | Your question text |
| Photo / Screenshot solve | Groq or OpenRouter | The image or screenshot you choose to submit |
| Sequence check | OEIS (oeis.org) | The number sequence you enter |
| Recent papers | arXiv.org | A request for recent papers |

These services have their own privacy policies, and their handling of data is not controlled by us. Please do not enter or submit personal or confidential information in questions or screenshots.

If you use the "Use My Own API Key" option, your key is used only to send requests to Groq or OpenRouter from your device.

## 4. Data stored on your device

Your question history, settings and any exported files (`.md`, `.pdf`) are stored only on your own PC. You can delete the history at any time with the "Clear History" button, or by uninstalling the app.

## 5. Math computation

Calculations made with SymPy and proof checking with Lean 4 run locally on your device and do not send data anywhere.

## 6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

## 7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
